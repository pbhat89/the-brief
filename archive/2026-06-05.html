Two minutes of thinking

Type: Quantitative

A bat and a ball together cost $1.10. The bat costs exactly $1.00 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?

How to approach: resist the first number that comes to mind and write out two equations — one for the total, one for the price difference — then solve algebraically.

Show answer

The ball costs $0.05 (5 cents). Let ball = x; then bat = x + $1.00. Adding them: 2x + $1.00 = $1.10, so 2x = $0.10 and x = $0.05. The bat costs $1.05; total $1.10. Most people instinctively say $0.10, which would make the total $1.20 — that’s the trap.
