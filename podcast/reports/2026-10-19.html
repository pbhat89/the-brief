1 / @AnthropicAI

Anthropic proposes a share-funded Public Benefit Fund

Anthropic plans to allocate 5% of its shares, subject to stockholder approval, to a wholly owned public-benefit fund. For each 50% increase in valuation above a baseline, the board may allocate up to another 2%. Governance and operating milestones are still to be detailed.

Why it matters: A concrete proposal for sharing AI-created value, including potential responses to labor disruption, rather than only general responsible-AI promises.

Company announcement, not evidence of money already distributed or independently measured benefit. The roughly 20% figure combines this plan with prior founder and employee commitments; it is not the fund's starting allocation. No IPO valuation verified or used.

2 / @rauchg

Vercel makes agent-session cost and caching visible in AI Gateway logs

A new Sessions view groups an agent's requests into conversations, exposing cost, models, providers, cache-hit rates, duration and turn-by-turn routing. Session affinity keeps later turns with the provider that handled prior turns, to reuse its prompt cache.

Why it matters: Helps teams investigate agent behavior and understand spending across multi-turn workflows, an operational concern as AI moves into production.

Verified against the vendor changelog, not an independent benchmark. No quantified cost reduction or reliability improvement established. Session affinity defaults to text/chat; explicit session IDs can be supplied.

One useful fact · Evergreen

Why fresh pineapple changes jelly

Fresh pineapple can stop gelatin jelly setting because bromelain breaks down protein chains. Canning heat deactivates the enzyme, so canned pineapple behaves differently.
