Singapore

Education Minister Desmond Lee says national examination papers may be rescheduled if the next day’s 24-hour PSI forecast reaches the Hazardous range, above 300. Mothership’s report of Friday’s press conference says examinations otherwise take place in enclosed, air-conditioned venues with air purifiers. This is a contingency, not an announcement of postponement; changes would come through the examinations board and schools.

Examination haze contingency

At the Port Workers Union’s 80th anniversary on Saturday, Senior Minister Lee Hsien Loong warned that rival ports are catching up with Singapore’s technology and stressed retraining workers as automation changes jobs. Tuas Port is planned to handle 65 million standard containers a year when fully completed in the 2040s.

Thinker perspectives and access
