One curious fact

Blue light around a Martian sunset

Martian sunsets can show a blue glow because fine atmospheric dust lets blue light penetrate more efficiently than longer wavelengths. NASA rover imagery documents the effect.

NASA · 17 November 2021 · Evergreen

Do not imply the entire sky is blue or every sunset equally vivid; the linked image has a muted blue effect because of less dust.
