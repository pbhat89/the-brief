Spend experiment budgets on more distinct settings

Karpathy explains that random search can explore consequential settings more efficiently than a rigid grid when a model is sensitive to only some settings.

Editorial application: In a bounded AI experiment, compare configurations within safe predefined ranges against the same evaluation set, instead of wasting repeated trials on settings with little effect. The nine-trial example is editorial.
