One expert idea · Evergreen

Prove the small pipeline before scaling

Evergreen April 25 2019: prove a tiny pipeline works before scaling. Distinguish a wiring check from performance on unseen inputs. Karpathy recommends fitting a tiny batch, even two examples, to expose pipeline faults before adding complexity. Learning those known answers is a wiring test, not proof of generalization.

Andrej Karpathy · 2019-04-25

Test a document workflow with two invented known-answer inputs before large-scale integration; then evaluate unfamiliar cases separately.
